DO Card is a physical NFC card product designed for self-imposed digital focus. The NFC card is the core product, similar to how AirTag requires a physical tag or Apple Watch accessories require a watch.

How the app works:
- Users pair their personal DO Card (NFC card) during onboarding
- They select distracting apps to block, then tap the DO Card to start a focus session
- To end the session early, they must physically tap the DO Card again
- This intentional physical friction is the core value proposition — it prevents impulsive unblocking

For review without a DO Card:
- A "Pair later" skip button is available on the NFC pairing page — tap it to proceed through onboarding
- The app uses NFCTimerBlockingStrategy by default, which auto-unblocks when the session timer expires
- Users are never permanently stuck — all sessions have a defined end time
- Screen Time authorization can be revoked at any time via iOS Settings > Screen Time

Technical details:
- Uses FamilyControls with .individual authorization (self-focus, not parental control)
- All data stored locally on-device only — no server, no tracking, no third-party SDKs
- NFC is declared in UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities — only NFC-capable devices can install
- Privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares UserDefaults usage under reason CA92.1

Demo account: No login required. The app is fully offline.
